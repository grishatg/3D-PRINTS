## Summary (short description)
A slim 65 × 13.5 × 88 mm book-style case for six rollies (up to 6 × 60 mm), with two filter-tip bays. It closes with two magnets sealed inside the print: the printer chimes and pauses for each one. The hinge pin is a length of 1.75 mm filament. No glue, no screws. Three ripple designs, one plate, about 2¾–3 h on an A1.

---

## Description

**A pocket case that clicks shut and feels good in the hand.** It opens like a book: a grey cover on a filament-pin hinge with a yellow hex sleeve, over a black tray with six vertical channels. Two hidden magnets give it a firm close. A yellow datum line sits at the golden section and wraps round the opening edge, and yellow ripples run edge to edge across both faces. The yellow initials **A·B** are inset on the bottom end.

### Pick your ripple
| Profile | Look |
|---|---|
| **A · Flow** | Vertical ripples that build toward the opening edge |
| **B · Rings** | Rings spreading from the clasp, like a stone dropped in water |
| **C · Topo** | Contour lines of a gentle landscape |

The case, the magnets and the print time are the same in every profile; only the ripple pattern changes.

### Features
- **Holds:** 6 rollies up to 6 mm × 60 mm in their own channels, plus 2 filter-tip bays.
- **Magnets sealed inside the plastic.** You drop each one in when the printer pauses, then it prints over them. Nothing to glue, nothing to fall out.
- **Filament hinge:** a 1.75 mm filament pin through printed knuckles, with a yellow hex sleeve on the spine.
- **Comfortable to hold:** rounded edges with a 45° lead-in at the bed, so they print cleanly without supports.
- **Clean colour:** all the yellow on the outside is only 4 layers deep, so there are no extra colour swaps for the ripples.
- **One plate, print by object,** sized for the Bambu Lab A1 + AMS lite. There's no prime tower, and every part is under 25 mm tall.

### What you need
| Item | Qty |
|---|---|
| Round magnets **Ø9.53 × 2.53 mm** (often sold as 9.5 × 2.5 mm; N35 or stronger) | 2 |
| 1.75 mm filament for the hinge pin | ≈ 87 mm |
| PETG in **grey / black / yellow** | AMS slots 1 / 2 / 3 |

### Printing
1. **Dry your PETG first,** 65 °C for 4–6 h. Damp PETG strings and blobs.
2. **Slicer settings:** open the profile and keep the project settings when Bambu Studio asks. Map Filament 1 / 2 / 3 to your grey / black / yellow slots. If your spools have no Bambu RFID tag, map them in the Print dialog instead of pressing Sync AMS.
3. **Print sequence:** **By object**, on the **Textured PEI** plate. The 4 mm brim keeps the flat parts down; peel it off afterwards.
4. **Magnets:** before you start, stick the two magnets together and mark both faces that touch.
   - **Pause 1, the cover** (around screen layer 25): drop a magnet into the round pocket, **marked face up**, press it flat, then press **Resume**.
   - **Pause 2, the tray** (around screen layer 82): same again, **marked face up**, then **Resume**.
   - The next layers seal them in. With both marked faces up, they attract when the case closes.
   - **No pause?** Pause by hand as soon as the screen shows layer 25 (cover) or 82 (tray). The pocket is still open at that point.
5. **Hinge:** slide the yellow sleeve between the cover knuckles, push the filament through from the top, and trim it flush. A quick touch of heat on each end flares it so it stays put.

### Print profile
- **Base:** 0.16 mm High Quality (A1), PETG HF filament profile, 240 °C nozzle, 70 °C plate.
- **Strength:** 100% infill, 4 walls, 6 top / 5 bottom shells, concentric top surfaces.
- **Plate setup:** 4 mm outer brim, no supports, no prime tower.
- **Time:** about **2 h 40 m** sliced, about 2¾–3 h with colour changes and the two pauses. 47 colour swaps.
- **Filament:** about 14 g grey, 33 g black and 2 g yellow, plus purge.

### Notes
- **Magnet fit:** the pockets are Ø9.85 mm and print at about 9.7 mm, so 9.5 mm magnets drop in. For thicker magnets (3 mm), the pockets are too shallow: the nozzle would hit the magnet.
- **Snap:** about 1 mm of plastic covers each magnet, giving a firm, positive close.
- **Material:** PETG is recommended. PLA will work, but the hinge and the edges wear faster.
